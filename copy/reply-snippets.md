# Reply snippets — paste, personalize the one variable, send

A human sends every one of these. The sequence pauses the moment any reply lands.

## "Interested / tell me more" (Positive)

> Great — simplest next step is a 15-minute fit call: I'll bring 10 accounts we'd target for {{Company}} and you tell me if they're right. {{booking_link}} — or reply with two times that work and I'll take whichever.
>
> One question to make it useful: who's your single best customer today, and did they come inbound or out?

## "Just send info" (Positive-lite — don't dodge, don't dump a deck)

> Happy to. Rather than a deck, I'll send the thing that proves it either way: 10 named accounts in your ICP with the trigger and source for each. Takes me a day. If the 10 are wrong, you've lost nothing; if they're right, we should talk. What's the best address for it?

## "We already have a vendor / agency" (Objection)

> That's genuinely fine — if the meetings are flowing, don't change anything.
>
> Two questions worth asking them, though: what's the verify rate on contacts before they're sequenced, and does every account come with a documented reason-to-buy-now? If both answers are strong, keep them. If not, that's the gap we fill. Want the 10-account sample as a benchmark?

## "Too expensive" (Objection)

> Fair — worth being precise about the comparison, though. The alternative isn't $0; it's a founding AE at market OTE plus 4–9 months of ramp, or your own hours, which are the most expensive on the cap table.
>
> If the budget genuinely isn't there this quarter, no push. Would it be useful to revisit once the {{round}} money starts turning into headcount plans?

## "Ping me next quarter" (Nurture)

> Will do — noted for {{month}}. So I don't come back with something useless: what changes between now and then? (Hire landing, launch, board meeting?) I'll tailor what I bring to that.
>
> Meanwhile the 10-account sample offer stands, zero strings.

## "Not the right person" (Referral)

> Appreciate you telling me — most people just archive. Who owns pipeline at {{Company}} right now? Happy to be introduced or to write to them cold and leave your name out of it entirely, whichever you prefer.

## Unsubscribe / angry (suppress immediately)

> Done — you won't hear from me again. Apologies for the noise.

*(Suppress across all channels the same hour. No save-the-sale line, ever.)*

## OOO (auto-handling)

Pause the thread, reschedule the next touch for 2 business days after their stated return date. Do not restart from email 1.

## Bounce

Remove the address, mark `invalid` in the sheet, attempt one alternate **public** source; if none, the account becomes LinkedIn-only. Never guess address patterns.
