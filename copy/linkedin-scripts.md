# LinkedIn scripts — parallel track (not a copy of email)

Rules: assisted, manual research only — no bulk scraping or automation. Catch-all and unverified-email accounts go LinkedIn-**first**. If a connect is ignored, stop; never pitch inside the connection request.

## Step 1 — Day 0: view profile, follow company page. No note, no request.

## Step 2 — Day 1–2: connection request (≤300 characters, zero pitch)

**Founders (Sequence A personas):**
> Congrats on the round — read the {{publication}} piece. I research early-stage GTM motions for a living and your category ({{their_category}}) keeps coming up. Would like to follow your build.

*(~210 characters. No ask, no link.)*

**GTM hires (Sequence B personas):**
> Saw you took the first commercial seat at {{Company}} — that job is its own genre. I spend my week in early-stage pipeline work; happy to swap notes on what's working, no agenda.

*(~180 characters.)*

## Step 3 — if accepted: one value note (still not a pitch)

> Thanks for connecting. One thing I noticed doing research in your space: {{one specific, true observation — e.g. "three of the big {{account_type}} players posted RevOps roles this month, which usually precedes tooling decisions"}}. No response needed — figured it's more useful in your hands than mine.

Then wait. If they engage, move to a real conversation. If email later verifies, the email sequence may start — but reference nothing from LinkedIn unless they replied.

## Step 4 — if ignored after 14 days: stop. Log it. No follow-up DM, no email escalation on the back of a dodged connect.
