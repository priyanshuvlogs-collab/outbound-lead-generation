## Email 2 — Day 3 — the ramp math

**Subject:** `research vs selling`

Rough split we keep seeing for first GTM hires: most of the week goes to finding and checking accounts, a fraction to actually talking to buyers. Nobody hired you for the first part.

If the research half showed up done every Monday — accounts, triggers, verified contacts, source links — what would your week look like by Friday?
