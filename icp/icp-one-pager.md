## ICP paragraph

Venture-backed B2B software companies, pre-seed through Series A ($1M–$40M raised, roughly 3–60 employees), headquartered or expanding in US / Canada / UK / EU / AU-English, that announced funding within the last 60 days and whose announcement explicitly earmarks capital for "go-to-market," "market expansion," or "customer acquisition" — but who have not yet built a sales team. The buyer is the founder/CEO currently doing founder-led sales; the champion is the first GTM hire (head of growth, founding AE) if one exists. They sell to identifiable, named-account markets (enterprises, warehouses, insurers, hotels, healthcare orgs, other software companies), which makes trigger-based outbound the correct motion for them.

## 3 jobs-to-be-done the buyer already has

1. Turn the fresh round into pipeline the board can see by next quarter.
2. Decide whether to hire a founding AE now or buy pipeline first.
3. Enter a new market (US/UK/EU) with zero existing brand or list there.

## 3 pain phrases they would actually say

1. "I'm the entire sales team and I'm also shipping product."
2. "We raised to grow but I don't want to hire an AE before we have a repeatable motion."
3. "Our market is like 400 companies — I can't waste any of them on bad emails."

## 3 disqualifiers

1. Sells outbound/sales-automation software themselves (competitor — e.g. Wayy.ai).
2. Consumer or prosumer product (Medly AI, Pally).
3. Mega-round with enterprise logos already ($60M+ seed, F500 customers — Corma, Rillet): they will hire in-house and drown in vendor inbound.

## Offer wedge

**Why this product:** they raised money earmarked for GTM but the founder is still the only seller; the alternative (founding AE) is a slow, expensive, high-variance hire.
**Why now:** the trigger is public and fresh — the funding note itself says the money is for customer acquisition. In 90 days the moment passes: they'll have hired someone or stalled.
**Why reply:** the ask is a free, account-specific sample (10 named accounts in their ICP), not a meeting.
**Status quo being replaced:** founder's own LinkedIn DMs + a spreadsheet; or a premature founding-AE hire; or a generic lead-gen agency selling volume.
